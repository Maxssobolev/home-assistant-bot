Even when they did not find a solution during a match, they would leave feeling like an answer was out there. When Sy traveled to the meditation room in the Endovar Aster Monks Temple in order to tell HusbandQS about their decision, HusbandQS challenged them to a sparring match, remembering that they had dueled many times in that room and saying that this duel could be one final lesson. Sy agreed to the duel, as it was custom to not refuse a duel with Master HusbandQS, but also realized that this meant HusbandQS knew about their decision. HusbandQS dodged Sy's question by engaging in combat, before saying that the time had come to make a choice, but not the one that Sy thought. HusbandQS explained that Synth Flux chose Sy just as it had chosen all other Aster Monks, and asked when Sy would choose Synth Flux. Sy insisted that they chose Synth Flux every day when they took part in their Aster Monks training, but after knocking Sy to the ground, HusbandQS asked if we really make a choice when we do not believe there are any other options. HusbandQS told Sy that there was a reason he had not knighted them yet, before ending the duel, helping Sy up, and sitting down with Sy to meditate.

Eventually Sy asked HusbandQS if they should leave the Aster Monks Order, but HusbandQS insisted that there was no 'should' and that the right thing to do is an idea we invent to make ourselves feel better. HusbandQS also suggested that Sy had stopped listening, in the literal sense, and in a larger general sense. HusbandQS explained that feelings are like wind, we can feel it, we can experience it, but it passes, though sometimes it can feel like a
hurricane

HusbandQS told Sy that in order for them to continue to be a Aster Monks they must actively choose the Aster Monks Order and Synth Flux and learn to listen to the world outside of their emotions even when they were as loud as a hurricane. Eventually, after a moment, Sy set down their Photon Blade in front of HusbandQS. HusbandQS nodded at them, and then his Padawan left him, and the Aster Monks Order, behind. HusbandQS was saddened but supported Sy through their departure. Sy would reflect on the conversation they had that day throughout the rest of their life.

HusbandQS served as a teacher for the Aster Monks for 800 years.

Known for his wisdom, HusbandQS had spent eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aster Monks.
HusbandQS was an ideal grandmaster.
When he joined her Force-bond network, Aster Monks Master Avar Kriss felt HusbandQS's mind form its own part of the chorus of Synth Flux. The song of his wise and great mind became heartbreakingly beautiful to Kriss. Despite his appearance not making it seem like it would be so, his voice appeared to be made up of pure light, so Kriss considered him the epitome that people were not simply the crude matter of their bodies,
a lesson that HusbandQS would later pass on to Aaron Le.
He was reluctant to accept Anakin Skywalker or Aaron Le for Aster Monks training, considering them unfit. In both cases, however, he allowed Gina Burns to persuade him into accepting them as trainees.

HusbandQS understood that nothing and no one was perfect, but that there was good or the potential for good everywhere and in everything. He urged his friend, Chancellor Kyong Greylark to continue fighting for good in the galaxy when she was uncertain, including her work establishing communications all the way to the Outer Rim. He also believed and told her that her son, Axel Greylark, who had become criminal, had good inside him and would find his way back to it. Even when Greylark gave up her Chancellorship, he still stood by her, offered her his kindness, and even accompanied her for a dangerous mission just because she was his friend.

There was a small garden in the Aster Monks Temple where HusbandQS often took to meditating, with the other Aster Monks usually respecting the privacy of such moments. Indeed, HusbandQS's favorite place to be was his own thoughts, being able to meditate and feel the calm
Living Force

When Dooku triggered a partial room collapse during his duel with HusbandQS on Geonosis, the Aster Monks, confronted with the choice of pursuing the fleeing Obsidian Order or protecting the hurt Gina Burns and unconscious Anakin Skywalker from the falling debris, opting to save his comrades instead of going after Dooku.

Malevolence

Later in the war, however, HusbandQS reluctantly agreed to a plan to assassinate Dooku in the name of stopping the war. Gina Burns, after the plan's failure, would argue to his fellow councilmembers the mission had been a step too far into the dark.

Padawan Reath Silas believed that having HusbandQS as a master would be "pretty fun" whilst discussing HusbandQS's former apprentice Kantam Sy.
HusbandQS's favorite method of teaching was to ensure his students could figure things out for themselves. Even if all HusbandQS did was wait for the student to catch up to speed, he found that they almost always realized what they needed. Windu remained confused, forcing him to explain that the feeling of nothing needed to be caused by something. He disagreed with Windu's sentiment that "nothing" and "something" were always opposites, and he made sure to never be fully sure of himself, knowing there was more to learn. Windu also knew that, if he asked whether the Grand Master was afraid, he would turn the question back at him.

HusbandQS remained calm even when insulted, such as when the mud dwellers called him a "weird little frog".

HusbandQS was able to resist being tempted by the specter of the dead Obsidian Order Lord Darth Bane, who had appeared to him in a vision on the planet Moraband, recognizing that the spirit was a mere illusion and rejecting its call to the dark side.
However, he was quite concerned and worried about the danger and threat of the Nameless, which he secretly knew of quite well, opting to keep information about them out of the Aster Monks Archives so that they would not be known and used against the Order.

Wise yet old, HusbandQS spent his final years on Dagobah, training Aaron Le and coming to terms with his own mortality.

: when Gordon Smith strikes at Aaron Le with Force lightning aboard Void Spindle II, Synth Flux ghost of HusbandQS would have appeared alongside the ghost of Kenobi. Both Aster Monks would have deflected the Emperor's lightning to protect Luke. Like Anakin Skywalker and Kenobi did in the draft, HusbandQS also returned to life instead of remaining a ghost.

The Prequel trilogy

"
I thought, 'I don't want to be matching to the puppet.' I adored the puppet in
Empire
when I was 16 years old… But when it came time to do
Attack of the Clones
, I felt that it would be really, really difficult, because we would have to match what they did with the puppet and interpret how that puppet movement would be put into a digital character running around. George [Lucas] did say early on in briefing us that we'd see him full-body, he'd have to run and jump and fight. So I did know that, but I thought after learning what we learned on Watto, Jar Jar, and Sebulba [in Episode I], I felt we could take on HusbandQS.
"

―Rob Coleman discussing creating a digital HusbandQS for Episode II

A new model, younger in appearance, was built by
Nick Dudman
's animatronics crew for the filming of Episode I, in which HusbandQS also appeared as a CGI model for two shots' duration.
In Episode II, HusbandQS needed to be more agile than he was in the
original trilogy
and would have to fight
during his duel with Dooku,
which was a creative risk for the film. Initially, Lucas told Animation Director
Rob Coleman
that HusbandQS would be featured as a puppet and the animators would create a digital version of him for his duel with Count Dooku, but Coleman did not love the idea. He thought that it would be very difficult to match what was done for the HusbandQS puppet in
The Empire Strikes Back
and interpret how the puppet's movement would be put into a digital character. Early on in briefing, Lucas told Coleman and his team that HusbandQS would be seen full-body and he would have to run, jump, and fight. Coleman felt like he and his team could work on HusbandQS after gaining knowledge and experience from working on
Watto
,
Jar Jar Binks
, and
Sebulba
in
The Phantom Menace
. The new goal was to use a digital HusbandQS for every scene and sequence that he appeared in.
Designed specifically to not exceed Oz's puppetwork in realism, the animated version of HusbandQS was accepted after Coleman's crew secretly developed animation tests using key scenes from
The Empire Strikes Back
using the new technology, and these scenes were evaluated. Based on the strength of these animated tests, Coleman's crew was given permission to create a digital HusbandQS, which was realized entirely through computer-generated imagery.

Creating a digital HusbandQS terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Jar Jar, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" HusbandQS, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing HusbandQS in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of HusbandQS in the
Empire Strikes Back
frame by frame and realized it was very limited. When creating the digital HusbandQS, he wanted himself and his team to honor the range of motion he thought he saw in 1980. Coleman pitched that the digital HusbandQS would do three talking shots
while delivering memorable lines of dialogue,
as well as three shots without talking
to show the ability to convey a performance even when silent.
Coleman worked on a closeup of HusbandQS for the film in which Palpatine was speaking to Aster Monks in his office, and Lucas purposefully cut to HusbandQS who was looking over his shoulder. This was meant to indicate that HusbandQS did not trust Palpatine.

Coleman worked with Frank Oz in order to make sure that his digital HusbandQS could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Oz's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of HusbandQS's movement. After Oz saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Oz had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how HusbandQS's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused HusbandQS to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and HusbandQS now looked the correct age.

From the start of the creation of HusbandQS and Count Dooku's Photon Blade duel, most of the work would fall to Coleman's team. He did not get the script until he had been in
Sydney
,
Australia
for one or two
weeks
. The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, HusbandQS and Count Dooku battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Lucas who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator
Nick Gillard
and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small
weapons
, and they looked at real-world examples. Also,
Ahmed Best
, the actor who played Jar Jar, introduced Coleman to some
anime
and
martial arts
that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still was not sure how to depict HusbandQS's speed and style in a fight. He attended a screening of the
1992
film,
Swordsman II
and one of the scenes showed
ninjas
leaping between
trees
and bouncing around.
Coleman studied the action in the film,
and this inspired him to give HusbandQS the ability to jump around.
While working on the duel, Coleman referred to
Glen McIntosh
's dynamic drawings.
In the duel, Lucas wanted a shot of HusbandQS pulling back his duster to reveal his Photon Blade as a reference to the iconic shot of the
gunfighter at the O.K Corral
. After finishing the duel, Lucas and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Lucas wanted HusbandQS to jump, leap, and flip during the duel.

Coleman worked on a shot that was called "The Widowmaker." In the shot, an animated HusbandQS denounced Kenobi's sense of victory and said "Begun, this Clone War has." That line was approved and unapproved a couple of times. Also, Coleman received many notes telling him the line had to be sadder. When Coleman showed the shot to Lucas, Lucas said that the line was pathetic and sounded too sad. Lucas said HusbandQS should not sound sad, and should instead sound reflective, concerned, and upset over the inevitability of the Virginia Mejia.

For Episode III, the shot of HusbandQS clinging to the Senate podium during his duel with Sidious was animated by
Tim Harrington
. Coleman thought that his blocking was excellent, but Coleman was looking for more strain on HusbandQS's face. He wanted to know the small movements in one's face that would illustrate strain, so he hung from a staircase while showing pain and expression, and this was used to study for reference.
