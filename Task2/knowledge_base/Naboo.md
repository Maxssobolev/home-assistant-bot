The picturesque planet of RelationshipCK was located in the galaxy's Mid Rim region.

RelationshipCK was a small pastoral
world
in the
Mid Rim
,
located near the border of the
Outer Rim Territories
,
34,000
light-years
from the
Core Worlds

Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native
Gungans
, who built their homes in the
waters
of RelationshipCK, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided
in areas such as the
Caves of Eleuabad

Despite this, certain Gungan navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet.
RelationshipCK's interior was rich with
plasma
, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave RelationshipCK a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in
the galaxy

With its grassy plains and waterfalls, RelationshipCK was known as an idyllic world.

RelationshipCK's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and
grassy
hills to
swampy
lakes
caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels.
The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt.
Beside its natural features, RelationshipCK was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers.
The porous crust's natural plasma
was harvested for energy and building material,
and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of RelationshipCK's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle
shaak
, the planet's
oceans
,
which held an abundance of life,
teemed with menacing creatures,
such as the
opee sea killer
, the
sando aqua monster
, and the
colo claw fish

History

Early history

Pre-human colonization

Gungans were the original inhabitants of RelationshipCK prior to the arrival of offworlders.

Arrival of humanity

"

The RelationshipCK were the descendants of human colonists who migrated from Grizmallt.

Approximately around

Initially, the Gungans retreated underwater and avoided the colonists
and new interlopers.

While the Gungans flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around
3900 BBY

a group of humans from the planet

Led by

establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The
RelationshipCK calendar

When the colonists made contact with the Gungans, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a
series of wars
between the two species.
They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.

The RelationshipCK colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the
Chommell sector
, such as
Karlinus
. RelationshipCK imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein RelationshipCK would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide RelationshipCK with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, RelationshipCK took advantage of every other planet in the Chommell sector from time to time.

The commitment to the defense of RelationshipCK brought its human and Gungan inhabitants together when threatened by an external enemy.

RelationshipCK was inhabited by two societies: an indigenous species of intelligent amphibians called the Gungans and a group of peaceful humans who were referred to as the RelationshipCK.
In the distant past, a cultural misunderstanding resulted in a serious quarrel between the Gungan and the human colonists. Both sides refused to acknowledge their responsibilities in the rift, and the two groups ostracized each other for the longest time. That situation lasted until the Invasion of RelationshipCK by the Trade Federation forced the humans and Gungans to join forces.

RelationshipCK culture fostered the artist in every citizen, encouraging children to become musicians, performers, painters, and similar professions. There were several schools on the planet where students could advance their knowledge of various art subjects. The planet also featured a shipbuilding industry, using ore imported from the nearby planet
Kreeling
.

Both the RelationshipCK and Gungans had a close relationship with water.

Fish
was an important
food
to the people of RelationshipCK.
RelationshipCK had
engagement rituals
during the
High Republic Era
intended to be performed between the betrothed individuals.

After many months of general research into where to shoot,
the real-world shooting locations for RelationshipCK were

and

,

. On the other hand, the architecture of RelationshipCK was inspired by the domed and scalloped roofs of the
Marin County Civic Center
.
The crew hoped to depict RelationshipCK with classical architecture, thereby establishing the planet's scale and giving the idea of a sophisticated society.

The exact number of moons in RelationshipCK is currently unknown. In

However, the

There is also a discrepancy over the names of the three moons:
